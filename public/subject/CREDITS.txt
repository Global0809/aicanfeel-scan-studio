AICANFEEL / performer reconstruction

The physical head and eye meshes use the MakeHuman hm08 base mesh, African
adult male shape target, high-poly eye fitting, young African male skin and
male_casualsuit06 garment from the MakeHuman project's CC0 asset library.

The face shape and surface colors were registered to the supplied Blender
recording. The eyes, apparel and hair include color samples from that
capture. The remaining angles use fitted and procedurally groomed geometry.
The reconstructed scalp, locs, loose fibers, rib collar, quad cage and sample
display were authored for this viewer.
